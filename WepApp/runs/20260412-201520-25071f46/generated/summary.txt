--- Classes ---
  • Traumatic Brain Injury — A type of brain injury caused by an external force, often leading to significant morbidity and mortality.
  • Head Injury — An injury to the skull or brain, which can have severe consequences for the victim and society.
  • Mortality — The cessation of all biological functions that sustain a living organism.
  • Trauma — A physical injury or wound caused by external force or violence.
  • Secondary Insult — A secondary injury that occurs after the initial injury, potentially worsening the patient's condition.
  • Monitoring Data — Data collected from patients to monitor physiological parameters and outcomes in clinical settings.
  • Cerebral Perfusion Pressure — The pressure required to ensure blood flow to the brain, calculated as the difference between mean arterial pressure and intracranial pressure.
  • Intracranial Pressure — The pressure inside the skull, which can affect brain function and is monitored in patients with brain injuries.
  • Therapy — Treatment intended to relieve or heal a disorder.
  • Neurological Injury — An injury that affects the nervous system, including the brain and spinal cord.
  • Substance Abuse — The harmful or hazardous use of psychoactive substances, including alcohol and illicit drugs.

  • Core Monitoring Parameter — A mandatory physiological variable collected at minimum once per minute.

  • Respiration Rate — The number of breaths per minute, measured via impedance plethysmography or ventilator.

  • Temperature — Core body temperature measured at rectal, oesophageal, bladder, or skin sites.

  • Fluids — The administration and monitoring of fluids to maintain proper hydration and electrolyte balance.
