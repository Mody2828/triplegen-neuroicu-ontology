--- Classes ---
  • Intensive Care Unit — A specialized department in a hospital that provides intensive treatment and monitoring for critically ill patients.
  • Patient Data — Information collected from patients, which can be recorded manually and/or automatically.
  • Data Quality Assessment — The degree to which data is accurate, complete, and reliable for medical decision making.
  • Heart Rate — The number of heartbeats per minute, which can be an important physiological parameter in assessing patient health.
  • Sepsis — A life-threatening condition caused by the body's response to an infection, which can affect heart rate among other physiological parameters.
  • Patient — An individual receiving medical care or treatment, represented in the patient data ontology.
  • Session — A defined period during which patient observations and data are collected.
  • Timepoint — A specific moment in time associated with a set of readings in patient data.
  • Reading — A recorded observation value associated with a parameter being monitored.
  • Parameter — A measurable characteristic or variable being observed in patient data.
  • Observation — A recorded measurement or assessment derived from patient data.
  • Machine — A device used for medical evaluation, represented in the medical machine/sensor ontology.
  • Sensor — A device that detects and measures physical properties, used in medical evaluations.
  • Sensing Process — The process by which a sensor collects data from a parameter.
  • Measurement Capability — The ability of a sensor to accurately measure a specific parameter.
  • Error — A classification indicating a potential issue with recorded data.
  • Traumatic Brain Injury — A type of brain injury resulting from an external force, leading to a range of physical and cognitive impairments.
  • Hypotension — A medical condition characterized by abnormally low blood pressure.
  • Hypertension — A medical condition characterized by abnormally high blood pressure.
  • Noradrenaline — A hormone and neurotransmitter involved in the body's fight or flight response, often used in medical treatments to increase blood pressure.
  • Adrenaline — A hormone released in response to stress, used in medical treatments to stimulate heart rate and increase blood pressure.
  • Acceptable Range — A defined range of values within which physiological parameters are considered normal or acceptable.
  • Sensor Accuracy — The degree to which a sensor's measurements reflect the true value of the parameter being measured.

  • Treatments
