### WHAT IS AN ONTOLOGY RELATION?
An ontology relation (object property) defines a typed semantic link between two classes. The DOMAIN is the subject; the RANGE is the object.
Model the SCHEMA of the domain — not individual facts. Think:
'Every instance of [domain] can be linked via [relation] to an instance of [range].'

### RELATION EXTRACTION METHOD
Systematically examine class pairs and reason step by step:
1. Scan the text for sentences where two or more classes co-occur.
2. For each co-occurrence, determine: what is the semantic nature of the link?
   (measurement, treatment, assessment, outcome, composition, causation, etc.)
3. Decide which class is domain (subject) and which is range (object).
4. Assign a clear, descriptive relation label.
5. Provide an exact verbatim quote from the text as evidence.

Head injury has devastating economic and social consequences both to the victim and to the society that supports the victim. The incidence of serious head injury
is estimated at 1500 per 100,000 population per annum.
On a European dimension this translates into over a

Head injury is a leading cause of death in young males
and survivors have serious and long term morbidity. The
loss of employment to the victim and the stress and

When assessing head injured patients' outcome from

Recruiting patients from multiple centres will significantly

intra-centre variability in the management and treatment of
